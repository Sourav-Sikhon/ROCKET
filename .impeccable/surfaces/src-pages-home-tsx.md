# Rocket website: home and site-wide surfaces

Scope: the full public Rocket website. Visitor mode: **Persuade** on home; **Read** on service detail, notices, help, about.

Audience/job: Bangladeshi customers and prospects on mid/low-end phones finding how a service works, where to transact, whether a notice concerns them, and getting the app. Action: open an account or download the Rocket app. Constraints: PRODUCT.md truth rules, no invented fees, limits, offers or claims.

Direction history (user, explicit, binding): earlier builds (route line, editorial spread, bKash-structure, amber/purple) were each superseded. Current and final pin: **the user supplied a design mockup (saved at _research/reference/comp.webp) and asked for the site to be made exactly like it.** The mockup is the approved comp. Where the mockup's wording states things Rocket's official pages do not back, the user chose "exact layout, verified wording": same boxes, sizes and colours, with official-equivalent copy (listed in the final report).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A soft lavender, white-dominant Rocket website with a violet headline, a hand holding the phone surrounded by floating service chips and violet ribbons, the Shaheed Minar fading in behind, and a deep-violet footer. It follows the approved mockup section for section: hero, About Rocket, Why Choose Rocket, Our Product & Services carousel, Awards strip, footer.

OWN-WORLD: Colours sampled from the mockup: page wash #F9F8FD, lavender #EDDFFD / #ECE1FC, ink #0F0A2E, headline violet #6A03AF, primary button gradient #6C0CA7 to #4D0A80, footer #550D77 to #440E6D with a #310F4F bar, pastel service cards #FDE7E3 / #E9F6F6 / #F3ECFA / #EDF3FC / #FDF2E1. The official Rocket wordmark stays in its brand purple #8A288F. Manrope for Latin, Anek Bangla for Bangla. Pill buttons, 22-30px rounded frames, soft violet shadows, a thin tracked eyebrow with a short violet rule above section titles.

STORY: The visitor sees the headline and a phone in a hand, gets the account and learn-more buttons at once, reads a short About with a real photo and three facts, sees four verified benefits, browses all twelve services as pastel cards, sees the recognition Rocket received, and finds help and contacts in the footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo, seven nav items, language toggle, Download App pill), then on the left the eyebrow, "Rocket / Now Closer to You", one sentence and the two pill buttons; on the right a hand holding a violet Rocket phone with four floating chips (Send Money, Pay Bills, Mobile Recharge, More Services) and violet ribbons, the Shaheed Minar and a tree line behind.

FORM: seed: none (user-pinned: the user supplied the comp, so the roll was not run). Code-led build; the comp is the critique reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
